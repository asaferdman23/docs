## Your data

- **Not used for training.** Bizzclaw does not use your messages, documents, prompts or outputs to train or fine-tune AI models.
- **Not sold.** Your personal information and content are never sold, rented or traded.
- **Kept separate.** Each account's data, memory and connections are isolated from every other account.

## Your connected accounts

- You connect tools through each tool's own sign-in. Bizzclaw never asks for passwords in chat.
- Access tokens are **encrypted at rest** and are never handed to the sandbox where the assistant runs code.
- Gmail access is **send-only**. Bizzclaw cannot read your inbox.

## Human control

- Anything that changes data, reaches another person or spends money follows the [approval rules](/concepts/approvals).
- Payments, deletions, phone calls and code changes **always** ask, every time.
- If Bizzclaw has read outside content (an email, a web page) during a task, it confirms with you before sending anything to someone else, so hidden instructions in that content can't act for you.

## Isolation

Code the assistant runs, for example to analyse a spreadsheet, runs in an isolated sandbox with no access to your credentials or other customers' data.

## Opting out

If someone asks not to be contacted, that opt-out is enforced on every send.

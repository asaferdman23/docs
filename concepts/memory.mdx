Bizzclaw gets more useful the longer you work with it, because it remembers.

## What it remembers

- **Notes** about you, your business, the people you mention, your preferences and the corrections you've given it ("never book me before 10").
- **Your conversations.** It can search past chats to find what was said, decided or promised.

Memory follows you across channels: what you told it on WhatsApp is available in the app and in your Slack DMs.

## Seeing and changing memory

Just ask in chat:

- "What do you know about Dana?"
- "Forget my old office address."
- "Remember that invoices go out on the 1st, not the 5th."

When something changes, Bizzclaw replaces the old note rather than keeping both.

## Privacy

Your memory is private to your account. Bizzclaw does not use your content to train AI models. See [Security & privacy](/security).

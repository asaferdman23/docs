Bizzclaw is an AI chief of staff for people who run a business themselves. You talk to it on WhatsApp, Slack or in the Bizzclaw app, and it handles the operational work around you: your calendar, follow-ups, your CRM, scheduled reports and routine tasks across the tools you connect.

It works **for you**, one-to-one. It never chats with your customers on its own, and anything that changes data, reaches another person or spends money waits for your approval.

## What you can ask it to do

- **Run your calendar.** "What does Thursday look like?" "Move my 3pm with Dana to next week."
- **Keep follow-ups from slipping.** It remembers open threads and checks back on them.
- **Work across your tools.** Find a deal in HubSpot, a page in Notion, an issue in Linear or Jira, a pull request in GitHub.
- **Do things on a schedule.** "Every Monday at 8, send me what's on this week and what's overdue."
- **Research on the web.** It has its own browser to look things up and read pages for you.
- **Handle voice notes, images and files.** Send a voice note or a photo of a document and it works from that.

## Who it's for

- **Owner-operators, founders, freelancers and consultants** who are the bottleneck for their own business and want one assistant that knows them.
- **Small teams on Slack** who want an AI teammate that follows up and keeps everyone current without a status meeting.

<Note>
Bizzclaw is not a replacement for staff and does not run fully unattended. It is built to take work off your plate while you stay in control of anything that matters.
</Note>

## WhatsApp

During sign-up, choose WhatsApp and tap **Message on WhatsApp**. Your chat opens with a connect code already filled in. Press send and you're connected.

You can send text, **voice notes** (transcribed automatically), **images** and **documents**, and Bizzclaw can send files and images back.

## Slack

Click **Connect Slack** during sign-up or from your dashboard and approve the install.

- **Direct messages** are private to you.
- **Channel threads** are shared with whoever is in the channel. Requests made in channels always go through [approvals](/concepts/approvals); only your direct messages count as your own request.

You can disconnect Slack from the dashboard at any time.

## The Bizzclaw app

Open **Dashboard → Chat** in your browser. On a phone, add it to your home screen to use it like an app. Desktop apps for macOS and Windows wrap the same chat.

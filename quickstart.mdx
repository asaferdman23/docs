---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [agent.bizz-claw.com](https://agent.bizz-claw.com) and create your account. The free trial needs no credit card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where you'll work with it">
    Pick **WhatsApp** or **Slack**. You can add the other one later from your dashboard.

    - **WhatsApp:** tap **Message on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with a connect code already typed in. Just press send.
    - **Slack:** click **Connect Slack** and approve the install in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say hello">
    Bizzclaw answers in your language and starts getting to know you and your business. Tell it what you do, who your key people are and what usually slips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a tool">
    From the dashboard, connect Google Calendar first. It is the fastest way to see Bizzclaw be useful. See [Connections](/connections/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a real task">
    Try one of these:

    - "What's on my calendar tomorrow, and is there anything I should prepare?"
    - "Every weekday at 8am send me my meetings for the day."
    - "Remind me Thursday to follow up with Noa about the proposal."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prefer an app?

You can also chat in the browser at **Dashboard → Chat**, install it to your phone's home screen, or use the desktop app for macOS and Windows. It is the same assistant with the same memory wherever you talk to it.

## Free trial and plans

The free trial includes a one-time allowance of credits (AI usage). Your dashboard shows how much you have left, and Bizzclaw tells you once when you've used 80%. See [pricing](https://bizzclaw.co/pricing) for paid plans.
